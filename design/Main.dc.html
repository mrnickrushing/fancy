Il Processo

Bend, snap, stretch, fold

Four moves, in that order, every time. It is the whole method and it takes all day.

I

Bend

The dough comes together slow. Organic flour, water, and a starter that has been going a while.

II

Snap

Air gets folded in, not beaten out. This is where the crumb is decided.

III

Stretch

Out to the corners by hand, never pressed flat by a machine.

IV

Fold

Rested, dimpled, drowned in good olive oil, and into the oven.
