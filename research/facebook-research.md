## 10. Brand voice — what the website has to sound like

She has a very distinct voice. Copy should match it, not flatten it.

- **Warm and personal.** Speaks as "we" and "I" interchangeably. Signs off **"Love always, Oh! You Fancy Focaccia"**.
- **Emoji-forward.** ❤️ 💜 😋 😚 🙏 👩‍🍳 🥒 — heavy but never cynical.
- **Faith is part of the brand.** "FATHER IN HEAVEN", `#ThankYouLord`, 🙏. Keep it; don't sanitize it.
- **Community-first.** She promotes *other* vendors inside her own posts. "Thank you so much for shopping local 💜"
- **Playful.** "BEND N SNAPS N STRETCH N FOLDS", "Muah 😚", "Look at these cuties", "mamamakesthedough".
- **Gratitude is the recurring theme.** Nearly every post thanks customers.

**Recurring colors:** purple 💜 is her most-used heart, with red and blue. Booth packaging
is **kraft/gold paper bags** with wooden crates. That's a real palette to build from:
warm gold/kraft, olive/herb green, deep purple accent, off-white crumb.

## 12. Needs confirmation from Amanda

Blocking items for the website:

1. **Phone number** — nothing public anywhere.
2. **Which market, which days.** Brookings-Harbor (Wed + Sat) vs. Brookings Farmers
   Market (Saturdays). Also: does the schedule change in winter?
3. **Menu and prices.** No prices appear anywhere public.
4. **How to order.** Right now the only public channel is Facebook Messenger + the
   Gmail address. Does she want an order form, text ordering, or pre-orders?
5. **Shipping details.** She says she ships — where to, what it costs, what travels well,
   lead time.
6. **Delivery area.** Brookings/Harbor only, or wider Curry County?
7. **Instagram handle**, and any TikTok.
8. **Business structure and licensing** — LLC? Oregon domestic kitchen / farm direct
   marketing license? Affects what claims the site can make.
9. **Allergens and "organic" claim** — she says organic constantly. Certified, or
   "made with organic ingredients"? Wording matters legally.
10. **Logo / existing branding**, and her preferred spelling: "Oh! You Fancy Focaccia"
    (with the exclamation, as she writes it) vs. the Facebook page name without it.
11. **Wholesale** — The Dawg House buys from her. Does she want a wholesale enquiry path?
12. **Full name** for the About page, and whether she wants her photo on the site.
