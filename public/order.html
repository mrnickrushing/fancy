Prima di ordinare

A few useful things to know

  • Orders are requests until Amanda confirms availability and the final total.
  • For changes or cancellations, email info@ohyoufancyfocaccia.com as soon as possible.
  • Shipping is charged at a flat fee, shown in your total before you send the order. Delivery availability and timing depend on the destination.
  • Please disclose allergies in the notes; we will tell you what we can safely accommodate.
  • Pickup is at the Brookings-Harbor Farmers Market on Wednesdays and Saturdays from 9am; exact arrangements are confirmed by email.
